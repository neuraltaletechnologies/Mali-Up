## Introduction

Mali Up ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web platform.

**Important:** Mali Up is a **business management and record-keeping platform**. We do **not** hold customer funds, process payments, or act as a financial institution. We facilitate and record your business transactions.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use Mali Up, you provide us with:

- **Business Information:** Business name, registration number, tax ID, business type, location
- **Personal Information:** Your name, phone number, email address
- **Financial Records:** Invoices, customers, expenses, inventory, sales transactions
- **M-Pesa Data:** If you import M-Pesa transactions, transaction details (amounts, dates, parties)
- **Account Settings:** App preferences, notification settings, security settings

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
- **Usage Data:** Features used, screens visited, time spent in app
- **Crash Reports:** Error logs (never include financial transaction data)
- **Analytics:** Aggregated, anonymized usage patterns (no individual financial data)

### 1.3 Information from Third Parties

- **BRELA:** Business registration verification data
- **M-Pesa (via Daraja):** Transaction data you choose to import
- **Google Cloud:** Infrastructure logs (encrypted, Tanzania-resident)

## 3. Data Residency & Security

### 3.1 Where Your Data Lives

**All your data is stored in the Africa region on Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Storage).**

- Business data: Firestore (Tanzania/Africa region)
- Backups: Encrypted by Google Cloud
- Never transferred outside Africa without explicit consent

### 3.2 How We Protect Your Data

- **Encryption in Transit:** TLS 1.3 (industry standard)
- **Encryption at Rest:** Google Cloud encryption
- **Access Control:** Role-based access (you only)
- **Audit Logging:** All sensitive access logged
- **Regular Backups:** Encrypted backups stored securely
- **No Screenshots:** Financial data never shared via screenshots

### 3.3 App Security Features

Mali Up includes optional security features:
- **Biometric Lock:** Fingerprint or face recognition unlock
- **PIN Protection:** Optional 4-6 digit PIN code
- **Device Encryption:** Option to encrypt app storage

## 4. Your Rights Under PDPA

The Tanzania **Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)** gives you rights:

### 4.1 Right to Access

You can request a copy of all data Mali Up holds about you.
- **How:** Contact us at [privacy@maliup.co.tz]
- **Timeline:** Response within 30 days
- **Format:** JSON or CSV

### 4.2 Right to Export

You can download all your business data at any time.
- **How:** Settings > Data & Privacy > Export My Data
- **Formats:** JSON (complete), CSV (tables)
- **Automated:** Immediate download, no human review needed

### 4.3 Right to Deletion

You can request deletion of all your data (PDPA Article 19).
- **How:** Settings > Data & Privacy > Delete Account
- **Timeline:** Deletion within 30 days
- **Scope:** All business records, invoices, customers, expenses
- **Irreversible:** Cannot be undone (export first if needed)
- **Backup Period:** Backups deleted within 90 days

### 4.4 Right to Correction

You can update incorrect information in your account.
- **How:** Edit fields in Settings or Data sections
- **Immediate:** Changes reflected instantly

### 4.5 Right to Withdraw Consent

You can change what you consent to.
- **Analytics:** Opt out of usage analytics (Settings)
- **Notifications:** Disable push notifications (Settings)
- **Cookies:** Mobile app does not use cookies; web app shows cookie controls

### 4.6 Right to Data Portability

You can transfer your data to another service.
- **Export in standard formats:** JSON and CSV provided
- **No barriers:** We don't restrict exports or lock you in

## 5. PDPA Compliance Details

### 5.1 Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data for these lawful purposes:
- **Contract:** Running Mali Up (you agreed to Terms of Service)
- **Legal Obligation:** Tax compliance, BRELA verification
- **Legitimate Interest:** Improving app security and performance
- **Consent:** Analytics, marketing (optional, you can opt out)

### 5.2 Data Sharing with Third Parties

Mali Up **never** sells your data. We share data only when necessary:

| Party | Data Shared | Purpose |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Google Cloud** | All data | Infrastructure hosting, encrypted |
| **BRELA** | Business reg number | Verify registration on signup |
| **Safaricom/Vodacom** | Your consent | Import M-Pesa transactions (optional) |
| **TRA** | VAT reports (you initiate) | Tax compliance (you submit, not us) |
| **Authorities** | As legally required | Response to valid legal orders |

### 5.3 Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Account credentials | Until account deleted | Authentication |
| Business records | Until account deleted | Your business history |
| Audit logs | 2 years | Regulatory compliance |
| Backups | 90 days after deletion | Disaster recovery |
| M-Pesa imports | 24 months | Regulatory requirement |

### 5.4 Processing Impact

We don't use automated decision-making or profiling that affects you. All decisions (features, access) are non-automated.

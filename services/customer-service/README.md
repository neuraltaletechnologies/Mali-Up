# Mali Up Customer Service

Future backend contract for customer and CRM workflows.

## Status

- Not part of the Phase 1 backend implementation.
- Customer records are currently owned by the mobile app through Firestore abstractions.
- This service is the future home for deeper CRM logic, segmentation, and reporting.

## Future Responsibilities

- Customer profiles and history.
- Segmentation and tags.
- Balance tracking and notes.
- Contact import/export.

Part of the [Mali Up](../../README.md) suite.
